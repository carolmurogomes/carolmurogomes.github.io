About

Curiosity across disciplines. Insights beyond boundaries.

I have always been drawn to the connections between seemingly unrelated subjects. Whether exploring a financial statement, a literary narrative, or the operation of a complex system, I want to understand what the evidence reveals—and what questions it invites us to ask next.

My professional path began with military training and service in the Brazilian Navy, followed by hands-on experience in marine engineering and offshore operations. I also bring experience in small-business management, team coordination, and leadership in environments where precision, adaptability, and clear communication matter.

Today, I apply that multidisciplinary perspective to Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Business Analysis. I work with Python, SQL, Power BI, data modeling, statistical methods, and visualization to translate complex information into useful, accessible insights. I am especially interested in combining technical analysis with creative thinking and data storytelling.

My projects reflect that curiosity: from financial intelligence and scenario simulation for WEG to the intersection of literature, geography, history, and emotion in Les Misérables. For me, data is not limited to a single industry; it is a way to explore questions across disciplines.

I completed Le Wagon’s six-month Data Analytics Bootcamp in 2026 and am currently pursuing a Technology Degree in Data Science at UNINTER. I continue building independent projects and expanding my analytical and technical skills.
